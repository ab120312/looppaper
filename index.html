Questions

How do I set a video as my wallpaper on Android?

Install LoopPaper, tap Choose, pick any video or GIF from your phone, watch it loop in the preview, then tap Set wallpaper. You can also tap Share on a video in Gallery or Google Photos and pick LoopPaper.

Is LoopPaper free?

Yes. Setting any video, GIF or photo as your live wallpaper is free, with no ads, no watermark and no account. LoopPaper Plus is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited playlists, all 123 transitions and the extra effects. The 7-day trial only starts when you tap Start trial and never turns into a subscription.

Can my wallpaper change automatically?

Yes. Make a playlist of videos, GIFs and photos and LoopPaper switches between them on its own, with a transition between each one.

Does a live wallpaper drain the battery?

LoopPaper rests whenever your screen is off and can pause in battery saver, so it only works while you are looking at it.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your files stay on your phone. LoopPaper never uploads them, and everything it does to a photo runs on the phone.
